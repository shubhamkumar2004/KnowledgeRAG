Retrieval time: 0.08 seconds
LLM generation time: 6.15 seconds

ANSWER:
The Education Loan Scheme is available for courses such as studies in India and studies abroad. For studies in India, the quantum of loan is up to Rs.10 lacs, while for studies abroad it is up to Rs.20 lacs. The scheme does not require any collateral security; however, a maximum security limit is set at Rs.7.5 lacs. The margin required depends on whether the loan amount is up to or above Rs.4 lacs: if up to Rs.4 lacs, it is 5%, and above Rs.4 lacs, it increases to 15%.
